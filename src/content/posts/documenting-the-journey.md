**"The only difference between screwing around and science is writing it down."**
<sub>- Adam Savage</sub>


## Why?

### ***Because without a written, accessible record, knowledge is impossible to maintain.***


After years of building things alone, researching but almost never documenting what I discovered or the lessons I learned, I've now understood that sharing the thing is more relevant than building it in the first place.


---
### ***Because building upon the knowledge of the errors of the past, is the only way to improve what's already existing.***


In a society where we grow more and more isolated, sharing becomes even more important.

To me, it's not about having the brightest idea, or the best execution, is about making information and knowledge available for whoever needs it, collaborate with people like me, sharing and understanding from each other, now as in the future.


---
### ***Because without errors, we wouldn't learn a single thing.***


That's why I decided to make this blog, to share my journey into what i'm building and how, the steps and decision I take, and the outcomes of those decisions.

I hope some of this information may be useful for someone, have fun, and don't be afraid of making errors.
